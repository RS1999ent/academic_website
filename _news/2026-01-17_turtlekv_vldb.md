Tony's work on a dynamically tunable key-value storage engine
(TurtleKV) was accepted to [VLDB 2026](https://vldb.org/2026/)! This
paper explores how to utilize the write-memory dimensions of the RUM
space to dynamically improve read or write performance.  You can read
the ArXiV version [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.10714v2).
